## Original photography, rights-checked media

Every gear review, tutorial, and editing guide on this site is illustrated
primarily with my own original photography — the same photos featured in the
[Collections](/collections/favourite) throughout this site. Where a manufacturer's
own official product photo is used instead (common for gear reviews
specifically — using a manufacturer's promotional photo of their own product to
review that product is a well-established fair-use pattern), or a
software-tutorial screenshot is used for an editing guide, it's noted. I don't
use another reviewer's photos, stock photography presented as my own work, or
any image whose rights are unclear.

## Affiliate / commercial links

Some articles — particularly gear reviews and buying guides — may include
affiliate links (for example, to retailers where you can buy the camera, lens,
or accessory reviewed). If you click through and make a purchase, I may earn a
commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects which gear I choose to
review or how I rate it.
