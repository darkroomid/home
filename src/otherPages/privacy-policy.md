**This page is a functional starting draft covering the disclosures required by
Google AdSense, GDPR (EEA/UK), and CCPA/CPRA (California). Have it reviewed by a
qualified professional before relying on it for a live, publicly-served site.**

## What data we collect

- **Analytics:** we use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic (pages
  viewed, approximate location, device type). This does not identify you
  personally.
- **Advertising:** once approved for Google AdSense, this site serves ads via
  Google. If you consent, ads may be personalized based on your browsing
  activity; if you don't, you'll still see ads, just not personalized ones.
- **Cookies:** both of the above may set cookies. You control this through the
  consent banner shown on your first visit.

## Your choices — consent

Every visitor sees a consent banner before any personalized advertising or
analytics cookie is set — not just EEA/UK/Switzerland visitors, though that's the
only place it's legally required. You can change your choice at any time by
reopening the consent settings. No personalized ad request is sent until you've
made a choice.

## Your rights — California (CCPA/CPRA)

If you're a California resident, you have the right to know what personal
information we collect, to request deletion of it, and to opt out of the "sale"
or "sharing" of personal information (which, under CCPA, includes some forms of
ad personalization). To exercise these rights, contact us via the
[Contact](/contact) page.

## Third parties

We use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Google's own use of data is governed
by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can review
and adjust how Google personalizes ads across the web at
[Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: see our [Contact](/contact) page.
